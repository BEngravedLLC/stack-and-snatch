    Table Talk

    How to play

    Goal: be the first to bank $1,000,000 across rounds. You only score what's sitting in your stack at the end of a round.

    On your turn, do ONE thing:

    1. Make a set — play two matching cards from your hand, or one asset + one wild (Silver/Gold). Two wilds together don't count. New sets go on top of your stack, covering older ones.
    2. Grab from the discard — if the top discard matches a card in your hand, pair them into a set.
    3. Steal — show a card that matches someone's top set (or a wild). They can fight back with a match or wild, then you can answer, and so on. Whoever plays the last card wins the set plus every card played — so stolen sets grow!
    4. Discard — toss one card on the discard pile.

    Steal rules

    • You need at least one set of your own before you can steal.
    • Everyone's first (bottom) set is safe 🔒 — a player needs 2+ sets before you can steal from them.
    • Only the top set can be taken. Covered sets are safe until uncovered.

    End of turn & round

    After every turn, everyone refills their hand. When the deck runs out, keep playing until all hands are empty. Then everyone adds up their stack.

    Card values

    Tip: cards glowing PAIR can make a set right now; STEAL means a card can snatch someone's top set.
